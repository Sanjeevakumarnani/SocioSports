

## SUBJECT OF WORK (SOW)
SocioSports Website
SocioSports – Sports Networking & Community Engagement Platform
This project lays the digital foundation for India’s sports ecosystem, bringing together athletes, trainers,
institutions, events, opportunities, and communities on a single scalable platform.
## 1. Objective
To design and develop a responsive, scalable, and future-ready web platform for SocioSports, positioning it as
a complete sports ecosystem showcasing athletes, trainers, events & tournaments, jobs & opportunities,
communities, institutions, and social impact initiatives.
